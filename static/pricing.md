# Pricing - StashDog

Canonical pricing page: https://stashdog.io/pricing/

Last updated: 2026-05-22

## Free

- Price: $0/month
- Currency: USD
- Billing interval: Monthly
- Best for: Getting started with personal home inventory
- Limits:
  - Up to 100 items
  - 1 photo per item
  - 10 AI actions per month
- Features:
  - Photo-based item inventory
  - Basic organization
  - AI-assisted item actions within monthly limit
  - Mobile app access
- Signup: https://stashdog.io/download/

## Plus

- Price: $4.99/month
- Currency: USD
- Billing interval: Monthly
- Best for: Power users, active households, collectors, and people with larger inventories
- Limits:
  - Up to 1,000 items
- Features:
  - Natural language search
  - CSV export
  - Expanded personal inventory capacity
- Signup: https://app.stashdog.io/sign-up?redirect=%2Fupgrade%3Fprice_id%3Dprice_stashdog_plus_monthly
