# StashDog Canva Brand Kit

## Brand Kit

Brand name: StashDog

Logos:
- Primary icon: `static/round-logo-goggles.png`
- App/icon source: `static/round-logo-goggles.png`
- Social/Open Graph fallback: `static/lab1.png`

Colors:
- StashDog Yellow: `#FCD900`
- Yellow Hover/Deep: `#E6C600`
- App Black: `#0A0A0A`
- Card Black: `#171717`
- Card Hover: `#222222`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Muted Text: `#A3A3A3`
- Alert/Accent Red: `#FF4D4D`
- Success Green: `#22C55E`
- Error Red: `#EF4444`
- Slate Dark: `#0F172A`
- Slate Mid: `#334155`
- Slate Text: `#CBD5E1`

Fonts:
- Logo/wordmark: Chewy
- Headlines: Gabarito ExtraBold or Bold
- Body: Gabarito Regular or Medium
- Fallback/body if Gabarito is unavailable: Inter

Voice:
- Direct, practical, slightly irreverent.
- Core promise: know what you own, where it lives, and how to find it fast.
- Avoid: polished-home perfection, luxury storage, warehouse/business inventory language.

## Canva Styles

Headlines:
- Font: Gabarito ExtraBold
- Color: White on dark backgrounds, App Black on yellow backgrounds
- Use compact, benefit-led headlines.

Subheads:
- Font: Gabarito Medium
- Color: Muted Text or Slate Text
- Keep to one or two lines.

Badges:
- Yellow text on transparent yellow tint.
- Suggested Canva fill: `#FCD900` at 8-12% opacity.
- Border: `#FCD900` at 12-20% opacity.

Buttons:
- Primary: Yellow fill `#FCD900`, black text.
- Secondary: transparent/dark fill, white text, subtle white border.

## Recommended Canva Templates

### Instagram Square

Size: 1080 x 1080

Layout:
- Dark background.
- Small StashDog logo top left.
- Large headline left or center.
- Product screenshot, box label, or QR visual on right/bottom.
- Yellow badge for the topic or promise.

Reusable headline patterns:
- "Find it without opening six boxes."
- "Your stuff, searchable."
- "Moving boxes that remember what is inside."
- "A home inventory you will actually keep up with."

### Instagram Story / Reel Cover

Size: 1080 x 1920

Layout:
- Logo top.
- Big 3-6 word headline in the upper half.
- Phone screenshot or item/box visual in lower half.
- Yellow CTA strip near bottom.

CTA examples:
- "Start free"
- "Track it once. Find it later."
- "Download StashDog"

### Blog Hero

Size: 1200 x 675

Layout:
- Dark/slate background.
- Left: category badge, headline, short supporting line.
- Right: abstract inventory cards, QR label, search icon, or product screenshot.
- Bottom left: `STASHDOG` and `stashdog.io/blog`.

### App Feature Graphic

Size: 1600 x 900

Layout:
- Dark background.
- Feature name as H1.
- Three cards for steps/benefits.
- Use yellow for icons and key labels only.

Example structure:
- Snap photos
- Save locations
- Search instantly

### Partner / Use Case One-Pager

Size: US Letter or 8.5 x 11

Layout:
- Logo and title at top.
- One strong promise.
- Three practical benefits.
- Screenshot or QR-box visual.
- CTA footer with `stashdog.io/download`.
