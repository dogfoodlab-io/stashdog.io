## Post 3 — Insurance Vertical

### "Home Inventory for Insurance: What You Need, Why It Matters, and How to Make One"

**Target URL:** `/blog/home-inventory-for-insurance`
**Primary keyword:** `home inventory for insurance` — est. 2,500–4,000/mo, Medium difficulty
**Secondary keywords:** `home contents insurance inventory`, `how to document belongings for insurance`, `insurance home inventory checklist`
**Intent:** Informational / High trust
**Estimated word count:** 2,000–2,500 words
**Priority:** 🔴 High

#### Outline

1. **Intro** — The scenario no one wants to think about: fire, flood, theft. If it happens and you have no inventory, you are in trouble. Most people don't have one.
2. **Why your insurance company wants a home inventory** — Explain how claims work. Without documentation, you can only claim what you can *prove* you owned. Photos and records are your evidence.
3. **What your home inventory should include for insurance purposes**
   - Item description
   - Photo (ideally with serial number visible)
   - Estimated replacement value (not original purchase price — what it costs TODAY)
   - Purchase date / receipt (if available)
   - Serial number or model number
4. **The most common mistake people make** — Not storing the inventory somewhere accessible outside their home. If the house burns down, so does your laptop. Cloud = mandatory.
5. **How to build a home insurance inventory in one afternoon** — Step-by-step with StashDog. Start high-value (electronics, jewelry, appliances), work outward.
6. **High-value items that need extra documentation** — Jewelry, art, collectibles, instruments. May require a separate rider on your policy.
7. **How often to update your inventory** — Major purchases, after moves, once a year minimum.
8. **CTA** — "StashDog stores everything in the cloud. If disaster strikes, your inventory is safe and searchable."

**Tone notes:** Treat the reader as an adult who doesn't love this topic but needs to do it. Be direct about risk without being fear-mongery. StashDog is the tool, not the hero — the reader is.

**Meta title:** `Home Inventory for Insurance: A Practical Guide to Documenting Your Belongings`
**Meta description:** `If disaster strikes and you can't prove what you owned, your claim suffers. Here's exactly how to create a home inventory your insurance company will actually accept.`
**Tags:** `insurance`, `home inventory`, `documentation`, `how-to`
