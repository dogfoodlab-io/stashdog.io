## Community Participation Rules

- Do not spam Reddit, Quora, forums, or Wikipedia.
- Answer real questions with useful advice first; mention StashDog only when relevant.
- Prefer examples, checklists, and practical workflows over promotional copy.
- Link to the most specific canonical page, not always the homepage.
- Track every mention so future AI visibility changes can be connected to distribution work.
