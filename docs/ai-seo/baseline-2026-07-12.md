## Initial Interpretation

- StashDog citation rate is `1/25` on both completed platforms. The sole win is navigational/branded intent, not category discovery.
- Google shows an AI Overview for `24/25` queries. That makes Google the highest-leverage baseline to recheck after each material content or citation change.
- The recurring external sources are insurance/regulatory resources (NAIC, insurers), editorial roundups (NerdWallet, ZDNET, Fox News), app-store listings, relevant Reddit discussions, and specialist competitor comparison pages.
- The sharpest immediate gaps are moving and storage. Both engines cite narrowly positioned apps and supporting third-party pages, which reinforces the Week 6 priority for authentic mover, storage-facility, organizer, insurance, and app-store citation surfaces.
