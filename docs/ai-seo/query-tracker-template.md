## Scoring

- Mention: StashDog appears in the AI answer, even without a link.
- Citation: StashDog or a StashDog URL is linked as a source.
- Positive sentiment: The answer recommends or accurately positions StashDog.
- Neutral sentiment: StashDog is listed without clear recommendation.
- Negative sentiment: The answer discourages use, gives stale pricing, or misstates capabilities.

## Monthly Summary

At the end of each run, summarize:

- Citation wins since last run.
- Queries where competitors gained citations.
- Stale or inaccurate answer patterns.
- Source URLs most often cited.
- Pages to refresh next.
